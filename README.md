# HR Attrition Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition data to identify patterns and possible reasons why employees leave a company. The goal is to turn HR data into actionable insights that can help improve retention, workforce planning, and employee satisfaction.

## Business Problem
Employee attrition can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and affect team morale. HR teams need to understand which factors are most associated with employee turnover so they can make better decisions around retention strategies.

## Objectives
This project aims to:
- measure overall attrition rate
- identify departments or job roles with higher attrition
- analyze whether overtime, income, age, tenure, and job satisfaction are linked to employee exits
- present findings through clear visuals and business recommendations
